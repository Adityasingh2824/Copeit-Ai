## Founder review checklist

1. Confirm the source is official, current, public, and relevant to the stated product and route.
2. Confirm its licence or permitted-use position before copying any text into the private staging bucket.
3. Upload only a UTF-8 TXT, Markdown, or CSV extraction that preserves canonical URL, publisher, and date metadata.
4. A different verified user approves or rejects the uploaded source with a rationale; the uploader cannot self-approve.
5. Enable retrieval only after staged worker, citation, abstention, isolation, and evaluation checks pass.

This register is navigation metadata. It is not evidence, legal guidance, or an instruction to mark a source approved.
